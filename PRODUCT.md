# Product

## Register

product

## Users

One person, checking in on their own life. Two contexts — a five-second check-in
(log gym, log a drink) at random moments of the day, and a once-in-a-while reflective
glance at the week. Always on a phone, added to the home screen.

## Product Purpose

On Track answers one question weekly: are you doing the things you said you'd do?
Five metrics (delivery orders, gym, social events, alcohol days, substances) scored against
self-set targets, gathered mostly passively. Success = the app stays effortless
enough to keep using; v1 died from being demanding.

## Brand Personality

Calm ledger. Quiet, steady, judgment-free record keeping — the app is a fair witness,
not a coach. Channels Linear / Things: precision, hairlines, soft depth, restraint.
Copy is plain and kind ("Noticed quietly", "Tap if you drank today"), never gamified,
never scolding.

## Anti-references

- Coach energy: uppercase pressure, strike-counting, "don't break the streak" guilt
- Whoop-style data maximalism: rings, gauges, dense stat feeds
- Duolingo playfulness: mascots, flames, confetti, chunky borders
- AI-default SaaS: gradient heroes, glassmorphism, cream-and-terracotta, card grids

## Design Principles

1. **The record is the reward.** Progress rendered as texture (bars, filled dots),
   not fireworks. A hit week should feel settled, not celebrated.
2. **Five seconds, one thumb.** Every daily action reachable and completable without
   thought; reflection lives one tab away, never in the way.
3. **Notice quietly.** Passive detections are presented as observations, not
   accusations — the app saw it, recorded it, moved on.
4. **One accent, spent carefully.** Indigo marks action and completion only.
   Over-target is amber, and that's the entire emotional range.
5. **Earned familiarity.** Standard affordances, consistent component vocabulary;
   the tool disappears into the task.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA: body text ≥4.5:1 in both themes, visible focus states, reduced-motion
alternatives for all animation. Hit/miss never encoded by color alone (shape +
fill carries it). Both light and dark themes are first-class (opened at random
moments, day and night).
